My Journey & Mission

Why I built WealthCraft

🌱 The Beginning

As someone who started learning about personal finance with limited resources, I experienced firsthand how overwhelming and confusing financial planning can be — especially in our local context. Complex calculators, scattered advice, and tools that didn't understand PKR or local investment options made it harder.

💡 The Vision

WealthCraft was born from a simple idea: What if finance tools felt like a trusted friend — clear, honest, and always on your side? Every calculator, guide, and feature is built with that principle: empower, don't overwhelm.

🚀 The Commitment

I'm committed to keeping WealthCraft free, private, and ad-light. No hidden trackers, no data selling, no aggressive popups. Just professional tools that respect your time and your trust.
